# Layer8Culture — 2026-08-19

The news anchor is quiet but structural: Google handed the Agent2Agent protocol to the
Agentic AI Foundation — the same neutral home where Anthropic's MCP already lives. Two
open standards, two different jobs. MCP is how one agent reaches your tools. A2A is how
agents built by different companies discover each other and hand work across. Agents
stopped being something you own end-to-end and started being something that negotiates.

That paired with the Tech Thursday moment worth showing rather than claiming: a
sub-agent spun up inside a GPT-5.6 workflow came back running Claude. Not a bug —
sub-agents pick whatever model fits the job, across labs, without asking. Which is the
same lesson from a different angle: with 350+ serious models live and the open-weight
ones trading blows with the closed labs monthly, having a favorite is a beginner move.
The skill stopped being *which model* and became *routing*.

The day runs that arc — protocol news at dawn, the routing principle at midday, the
cross-lab proof in the afternoon, a free offline model that never touches the internet
at drive time, and a culture close on the UAE putting agentic AI across half its federal
services.
