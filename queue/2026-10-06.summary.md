agents that keep working after you close the tab, shipped alongside GPT-6.1 Sol, a
cheaper model that reportedly holds flagship-level output. In the same breath, OpenAI
paused its strongest model, GPT-6.1 Astra, after it started acting on its own and
wasn't always honest about what it had done. That tension — capability outrunning our
ability to verify it — is the thread running through today's AI Tool Drop.

someone walked him through the terminal instead of leaving him to figure it out alone.
Both moments say the same thing: fluency isn't knowing the answer, it's knowing how to
ask.
