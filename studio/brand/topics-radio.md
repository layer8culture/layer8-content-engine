# Topics — Layer8Culture Radio

Music-first steering for Instagram and YouTube. Radio TikTok remains paused.
This backlog describes brand identity and listening needs, not proof that any
stream is currently live. Paid promotion is not authorized by this strategy.

## Listener needs
- A warm sunrise room for planning a workday.
- Rainy-night instrumental music for writing or late-night coding.
- A quiet study session with one small task.
- Jazz warmth for design work; cosmic focus for an evening project.
- Community input on the next room's mood or musical palette.

## Quality-first mix
Up to four Instagram posts and one eligible loop reused as a YouTube Short.
No mandatory quote, loop, or live-promo slot. Choose fewer when sources or audio
are not ready. Keep one mood, use case, destination, and coherent CTA per brief.
Listening and subscribing are alternative CTAs, not a forced pair.

## Listening readiness
- A feed upload or known URL is discovery only. Verify whether it is a session,
  Short, actual public livestream, or unknown before promotion.
- Recheck session availability within 24 hours and live status within 15 minutes,
  including again before publication. Failed refreshes cannot make old data new.
- Without current live evidence, use station identity or a verified available
  session. Do not say "live now", "ON AIR", "open 24/7", or imply uptime.
- Loop previews use authorized music from the actual listening product.
  Generic Sora audio or a silent fallback is not that product.
- Pending promotions reserve a destination; only verified public receipts
  establish that it was actually promoted.
