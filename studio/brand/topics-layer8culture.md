# Layer8Culture — practical AI editorial steering

This is an evergreen audience-needs backlog, not a dated news feed. The runner's
explicit date, verified source bundle, and selected briefs govern each batch.
Do not revive old launch, pricing, adoption, or legal claims without new evidence.

## Primary audience
Working professionals and small-business operators learning practical AI.
Explain unfamiliar terms; a useful builder does not have to be a developer.

## Learning progression and durable questions
- **Brief:** turn a vague task into an outcome, input, constraint, and review rule.
- **Context:** redact a sample before asking for a draft; distinguish necessary
  context from confidential business/customer data.
- **Verify:** compare an AI meeting summary against the source, mark unsupported
  claims, and check owners/dates before sharing.
- **Apply:** draft a customer reply from a fictional example, then compare tone
  and factual promises with a short acceptance checklist.
- **Repeat:** save a successful prompt plus example input and a human review step;
  use a repeatable workflow only after a small checkable trial.
- **Evaluate:** record corrections/time on comparable tasks; do not claim saved
  hours or productivity gains without an actual measurement.

## Source-backed opportunities
- Tech Thursday lessons only from indexed owned recordings with a recorded date
  and exact cue; no latest-file-by-mtime rule or forced transcript mention.
- Tool news only when it changes one audience decision and source age is verified.
- Weekly Field Guide catalog: select an explicit campaign brief, then teach the
  standalone lesson. Promote the public guide only after actual deployed-page
  and connected resource-delivery readiness is verified.
- Build-in-public examples need actual artifacts; generated visuals are illustrative.

## Quality-first ceilings, not quotas
Main platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube. Radio runs separately.
Up to five 8–12s cinematic TikTok masters, up to two eligible master families
reused on Instagram/YouTube, up to two Instagram carousels and one story.
No mandatory daily mix. Preserve the five viral pillars and ten canonical
categories. Rank usefulness, evidence, audience fit, novelty; hook scores alone
do not choose fan-out. One creative family counts as one lesson.

## Avoid
- Unsupported "everyone", "nobody", guaranteed results, or vendor cheerleading.
- Stale calendar news relabeled fresh when web retrieval fails.
- Instructions that depend on an unavailable guide or placeholder DM URL.
- Repeated lessons across pending/approved/published queues without a genuinely
  new action/result check. A renamed hook is not a new lesson.
- Claimed performance winners when lane/platform analytics are absent or sparse.
